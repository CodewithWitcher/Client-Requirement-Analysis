## 🚀 How to Use This Toolkit

### Step 1 — Understand the Parameters
Start with the **docs/** folder. Read the complete requirement guides to understand every parameter you should consider when scoping a project.

### Step 2 — Client Discovery Meeting
Use the **templates/** folder questionnaires during your first client call. These are structured question sheets that cover every aspect — from domain & hosting to maintenance & support.

### Step 3 — Estimate Pricing
Open the **excel/** pricing calculators. Fill in the client's requirements, and the sheet will help you build a price breakdown for the project.

### Step 4 — Send Proposal
Use the **word/** proposal templates to create a professional, branded document to send to your client with all the pricing details, scope, and deliverables.

### Step 5 — Track Progress
Use the **checklists/** to ensure nothing is missed during development, testing, and handover.

## 💡 Tips for Client Conversations

1. **Always start with "Why"** — Understand the business goal before diving into technical requirements.
2. **Document everything** — Use the questionnaires to capture every detail in writing.
3. **Be transparent about costs** — Break down pricing so clients understand what they're paying for.
4. **Set expectations early** — Discuss timelines, revision limits, and maintenance costs upfront.
5. **Offer tiers** — Provide Basic, Standard, and Premium pricing options when possible.
6. **Get sign-off** — Have clients approve the scope and pricing before starting work.
